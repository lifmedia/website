How we think
Video isn’t content. It’s how people decide who to trust.

Every film starts with one question: what should this make people do? Book the call, visit the site, apply for the job, remember the name. We plan, shoot and edit around that answer.

From a national broadcast to the shop down the street, the job is the same: tell the true story, beautifully, and make it work for the business.

We are a founder-led team of four. We film on the Sony FX3, a camera on Netflix’s approved list, and aerial drone coverage is part of every production. Most of our clients work with us month after month.

Jorge MelgarFounder & Creative Director
