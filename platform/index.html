What the Platform Does

One dashboard for all your social channels

LIF Media built this platform to serve our video production clients — giving their teams a centralized place to review, schedule, and publish the content we produce for them, across every platform they use.

Publish Video Content

Upload and publish approved videos directly to YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and TikTok on behalf of connected clients.

Schedule Posts

Plan and schedule content weeks in advance. Posts go live automatically at the times your team sets.

Client Account Access

Clients connect their own social media accounts via OAuth. LIF Media publishes only with the client's explicit, revocable authorization.

Publishing Status

Track the status of every upload in real time. Connected channel information — name and thumbnail — is displayed to confirm the correct account is linked.

Authorization & Data Access

Your accounts. Your control.

Account access is granted exclusively through each platform's official OAuth flow. When a client connects a channel, they see exactly what permissions they are granting before approving. LIF Media receives only the minimum access needed to perform the requested publishing action.

For YouTube, we use youtube.upload to publish videos and youtube.readonly to display the connected channel's name and thumbnail. We do not access watch history, playlists, subscriptions, or any other YouTube data.

Connected account access can be revoked at any time — directly through each platform's own permission settings (e.g., Google Account permissions for YouTube) — without contacting LIF Media.
