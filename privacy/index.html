Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

LIF Media Inc. Last updated: June 21, 2026

LIF Media Inc. ("LIF Media", "we", "us", "our") is a video production and social media management company based in Moncton, New Brunswick, Canada. This Privacy Policy covers our public website (lifmedia.ca) and our social media management platform (social.lifmedia.ca). It explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and your rights under Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

What we collect

When you submit a contact or project inquiry form, we collect the information you provide: your name and email address (required), and optionally your phone number, company name, budget range, service interest, and project details. We do not collect payment information, passwords, or sensitive personal data through this website.

Our website also automatically collects anonymized analytics data — including pages visited, session duration, device type, and general location — through Google Analytics 4.

When you or an authorized representative connect a social media account through our management platform (social.lifmedia.ca), we additionally collect: OAuth access tokens for the connected accounts (YouTube, Facebook/Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok), the connected channel's public profile information (name and thumbnail, used solely to confirm the correct account was linked), and the video content and metadata you upload for scheduling and publication. We access only the minimum data required to perform the requested publishing actions on your behalf.

How we use your information

We use the information you submit through our forms solely to respond to your inquiry and discuss a potential project. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

We use analytics data to understand how visitors find and use our website so we can improve it. This data is anonymized and not linked to individual identities.

We use OAuth tokens and connected account data exclusively to schedule and publish the video content that you or your authorized representative have reviewed and approved, on your behalf and with your explicit consent. We do not access, store, or share any other data from your connected social media accounts.

YouTube API Services

Our social media management platform uses the YouTube API Services. By connecting your YouTube channel through our platform, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. The use of information received from the YouTube APIs is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Specifically, we use the YouTube API to:

  • Upload and publish video content that you or your authorized representative have approved (youtube.upload scope).
  • Display your connected channel's name and thumbnail so you can confirm the correct channel is linked, and to show the status of your uploads (youtube.readonly scope).

We do not access, store, or share any other data from your YouTube account beyond what is described above. You can revoke LIF Media's access to your YouTube account at any time by visiting the Google Account permissions page.

Third-party services

This website and platform use the following third-party services that may process your data:

  • EmailJS — processes form submissions to deliver your inquiry to our inbox. EmailJS temporarily handles your form data on their servers. See the EmailJS Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics 4 & Google Ads — collects anonymized usage data and conversion tracking on this website. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube API Services — used by our social media management platform to upload and publish video content on connected channels. See the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
  • Meta (Facebook & Instagram) — used by our platform to publish content to connected Facebook Pages and Instagram Business accounts on behalf of clients. See Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • LinkedIn — used by our platform to publish content to connected LinkedIn Pages on behalf of clients. See LinkedIn's Privacy Policy.
  • TikTok — used by our platform to publish content to connected TikTok Business accounts on behalf of clients. See TikTok's Privacy Policy.
  • Calendly — if you book a discovery call using our Calendly link, Calendly collects your name, email, and booking details directly. See the Calendly Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudinary — serves images and media on this website. No personal data is shared with Cloudinary from your use of this site.

Cookies

Google Analytics 4 and Google Ads set cookies in your browser to distinguish visitors and track conversions. These cookies do not contain personally identifiable information. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, or by adjusting your browser's cookie settings.

Data retention

Inquiry emails delivered to our inbox are retained for as long as necessary to manage our client relationships and comply with legal obligations. Google Analytics data is retained according to Google's standard retention settings. We do not store form submission data on our own servers.

OAuth access tokens for connected social media accounts are stored securely and used only to perform publishing actions you have authorized. Tokens are deleted within 30 days of account disconnection or upon written request to contact@lifmedia.ca. Scheduled and published content metadata may be retained for client reporting purposes and is deleted upon client request.

Your rights under PIPEDA

Under Canada's PIPEDA, you have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Withdraw consent to the collection or use of your personal information (subject to legal or contractual restrictions)
  • Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contact@lifmedia.ca.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the website after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
